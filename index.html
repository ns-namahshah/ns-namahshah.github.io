01
SaaS

AI-Driven Hyper-Personalized ABX
Nurture Engine for Mid-Market & Enterprise

Demand Generation | ABX
Pipeline Lift
76%
Context

Pipeline growth was the mandate. The business needed scalable programs to nurture target accounts - moving them consistently through the funnel without adding headcount. Existing outreach was broad and manual; there was no infrastructure to engage the right accounts at the right moment with relevant, personalized content at scale. The goal: drive more pipeline through automation, personalization, and intent-led nurture.

Approach

Previous personalization had been at the industry level. This campaign went a layer deeper - personalizing at the individual account level, at scale, while remaining fully automated.

  • Identified target ICP accounts showing early to mid-funnel intent in 6sense - warm accounts not yet in active buying stages, targeted specifically for nurture
  • Accounts entering the campaign trigger an AI research assistant to run deep analysis - surfacing signals relevant to each account’s business, mapped to our product use case
  • Research outputs drive customization of 6 persona-aware emails per account, with each email linking to a dedicated account-specific landing page as the CTA
  • All sequences built and automated in HubSpot - new accounts enter the workflow and the entire motion fires on autopilot, no manual intervention required
  • Clay enriched accounts with firmographic and technographic depth to inform targeting and personalization
Outcome
  • Lift in monthly pipeline ARR ($1.77M → $3.12M) 76%
  • New pipeline ARR influenced $2.33M
  • Accounts moved into Decision / Purchase stages 801
  • Designed, launched & operational within 90 days

02
SaaS

Automated End-to-End
GTM Orchestration Workflow

Marketing Operations | Automated GTM Orchestration
SDR Time Freed
~90%
Context

SDR time was being consumed by manual prospecting - researching accounts, pulling them into the CRM, identifying target personas, enrolling contacts into sequences - leaving minimal time for actual outreach and calls. The goal was to automate the full process end-to-end, so SDRs could focus entirely on what moves pipeline: calling high-intent prospects.

Approach

I built an n8n automation workflow that orchestrates the complete intent-to-outbound motion - from signal detection to SDR-ready handoff - with zero manual steps in between.

  • 6sense surfaces accounts showing strong intent signals or in Decision / Purchase buying stages
  • Workflow checks if accounts are net new → if yes, auto-adds to Salesforce and HubSpot
  • Clay automatically enriches accounts with target persona-level contact data
  • Checks if contacts are already enrolled in Salesloft cadences → if not, auto-enrolls them
  • Slack alert fires to the assigned SDR with all relevant account and contact context
  • SDR’s only job: call
Outcome
  • Manual SDR prospecting time eliminated per intent cycle ~90%
  • Time from intent signal to SDR-ready handoff Minutes
  • End-to-end workflow: intent detection to enrolled cadence Fully automated

03
SaaS

AI Account Research Brief,
Auto-Generated & Delivered to SDRs

Sales Enablement
SDR Efficiency Gain
40%
Context

SDRs were spending significant time researching target accounts before sending a single outreach message - pulling firmographics, understanding the business context, and trying to connect it back to our product use case. The output was inconsistent across reps, slowed outreach velocity, and left SDRs under-prepared for calls. The fix had to live inside their existing workflow: Salesforce and Salesloft.

Approach

I designed a Salesforce-campaign-triggered automation that delivers a fully AI-generated account research brief directly into the SDR’s existing tools - the moment a target account is identified.

  • SDR adds an account to a designated Salesforce campaign
  • Trigger fires for an AI research assistant via API
  • Brief is generated - covering all relevant business signals mapped to our product use case, including context to develop targeted talking points for outreach and calls
  • Output is exported into the Salesforce account record and surfaced in Salesloft
  • SDR has all account research pre-populated and ready to review before their call
Outcome
  • Improvement in SDR process efficiency 40%
  • Research brief delivered after account is added < 15m
  • Consistency of account intel across the SDR team 100%
  • New tools added to SDR workflow Zero
